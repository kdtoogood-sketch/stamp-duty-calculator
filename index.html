Common questions

What is the stamp duty in Gurugram in 2026?

Inside municipal limits it is 7% for men, 5% for women and 6% for a man and woman buying jointly. Outside municipal limits it is 5%, 3% and 4%. It is charged on the deal value or the collector rate value, whichever is higher.

What is the registration fee in Gurugram?

Haryana charges a slab fee on the deed value, from ₹100 up to a maximum of ₹50,000 for property worth more than ₹90 lakh.

What is the collector rate (circle rate)?

It is the minimum value fixed by the Deputy Commissioner for each sector, colony and village. Stamp duty can never be paid on less than this value. The 2026-27 rates apply from 1 April 2026.

Is there stamp duty on a gift deed to family in Haryana?

A gift to a close blood relation has no stamp duty in Haryana, but the deed must still be registered and the registration fee is payable. Confirm the exact relation with the Sub-Registrar.

How much TDS is deducted when buying property?

The buyer deducts 1% TDS if the value is ₹50 lakh or more and the seller is a resident. If the seller is an NRI, TDS is 12.5% plus surcharge and cess on long-term gains (13% to 14.95%), or about 31.2% on short-term gains, on the full price unless the seller has a lower TDS certificate.

What is the stamp duty on a lease or rent agreement in Gurugram?

Up to 5 years it is 1.5% of the average annual rent, and 3% for 5 to 10 years. Leases of 12 months or more must be registered.
